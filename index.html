KAIWEN YANG / WORLDS & PLAY

Hi, I’m Kaiwen Yang. I created all five of these projects independently.

I turn an interest in knowledge, languages, and play into things you can open, browse, and interact with. From initial ideas to complete experiences, this collection brings together five explorations: a gateway to Latin American studies, tools for Spanish and Portuguese learning, an index of professional references, and little worlds for pixel characters and a faraway street stall.

I hope this space helps you discover both the work and an entry point you want to follow. Open a website, or spend a few minutes inside a little game.
